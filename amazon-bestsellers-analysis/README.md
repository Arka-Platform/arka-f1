# Amazon India Bestsellers - Quantitative Research Analysis

## Project Overview
This project conducts comprehensive quantitative research on Amazon India's November bestsellers dataset, applying statistical methodologies and data science best practices to extract meaningful insights.

## Dataset
- **Source**: `november_bestsellers_amazon_india.csv`
- **Records**: ~32,500 books
- **Time Period**: November (Amazon India Bestsellers)

## Research Methodology

### 1. **Descriptive Statistics**
- Central tendency measures (mean, median, mode)
- Dispersion measures (standard deviation, variance, range)
- Distribution analysis (skewness, kurtosis)
- Percentile analysis

### 2. **Inferential Statistics**
- Correlation analysis (Pearson, Spearman)
- Hypothesis testing
- Statistical significance testing
- Confidence intervals

### 3. **Market Segmentation**
- K-means clustering
- Feature standardization
- Segment profiling
- Market positioning analysis

### 4. **Price Analysis**
- Price distribution analysis
- Price range categorization
- Price elasticity indicators
- Market pricing patterns

### 5. **Rating & Review Analysis**
- Rating distribution
- Review count analysis
- Quality indicators
- Customer satisfaction metrics

### 6. **Format & Category Analysis**
- Format preferences
- Category distribution
- Format-price relationships
- Market trends

## Key Research Questions

1. What is the typical price range for bestsellers?
2. Is there a relationship between price and rating?
3. Which book formats dominate the market?
4. Who are the most successful authors and publishers?
5. What market segments exist in the bestseller category?
6. How do ratings correlate with review counts?
